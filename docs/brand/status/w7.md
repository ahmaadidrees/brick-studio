## 1. Audit — current Toy Room versus direction I

Reference: `direction-I-approved.png`, boards 01 / 06 / 07 / 08. Direction I is a cozy oversized desk in a **cool blue
room** lit by a **warm oversized desk lamp**, with a stack of **blue / coral / butter books**, a **wall clock**, a **ruler**,
a blue **pencil mug**, simple readable game shapes, and a warm-white baseplate. Palette: cornflower `#5888DA`, coral
`#F17861`, butter `#F3CA74`, ink `#263C51`, warm white `#F8F4EB`.

| Element | Current (`toyRoom.ts`, `ToyRoomWorld.tsx`, `toyRoomTextures.ts`) | Direction I | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Room walls | Sage-grey striped wallpaper `#b3c4c0` with cross motifs | Flat deep cornflower/navy blue walls, plain | **Change**: flat blue wall material, drop the busy wallpaper generator |
| Background / fog | Grey-mauve dusk `#6a7181` | Deep blue dusk | **Change**: blue fog/background matching the walls |
| Window | Bright white-blue panel + additive halo, cream frame | Night city skyline, dusk gradient sky, stars, dark frame | **Change**: one canvas "skyline" texture on a single plane (replaces two planes) |
| Desk | Oak `#b07a45`, brown slab/rails/legs | Warm honey/blond wood under lamp light | **Change**: warmer, lighter grain + slab colors; geometry unchanged |
| Desk lamp | Teal enamel `#2f8a95` / `#35a0ad` | Butter-yellow oversized swing-arm lamp with warm glow | **Change**: butter shade/base, warm-gold arm; positions untouched (`LAMP_*` are load-bearing for the beam, spot, colliders) |
| Books | Maroon / dark teal / mustard / navy | Blue / coral / butter / cornflower stacked hardcovers | **Change**: recolor spines to brand; simplify per-book decoration meshes (6 → 4) |
| Ruler | Sand `#e0b878` plank, 12 separate tick meshes | Butter ruler with ink ticks | **Change**: butter + ink, ticks drawn in one canvas texture (−12 meshes) |
| Clock | none | Wall clock on the back wall | **Add**: face (textured disc) + rim + 2 hands = 4 meshes, no animation |
| Mug | Cream mug, teal band, cold coffee | Cornflower "SMALL IDEAS BIG WORLDS" pencil mug holding pencils | **Change**: cornflower mug, pencils standing in it (3 thin cylinders); coffee disc removed |
| Giant bricks | Red / blue / yellow / green / orange | Cornflower / coral / butter (+ one green accent) | **Change**: recolor only |
| Crayons, eraser, sticky note, ball, dice, alphabet blocks, marble | Mixed off-brand hues | Brand hues | **Change**: recolor; crayon wraps reduced (5 → 3 meshes each) |
| Baseplate (`EXPLORE_PLATE_COLOR`) | Bluish grey `#b8c1bc` | Warm-white studded plate | **Change**: warm white `#e8e1d2` (studs stay on, clearcoat stays) |
| Lights | Ambient + hemisphere + warm key (shadow) + spot pool + cool fill + bounce directional | Cool blue fill, warm lamp key | **Change**: colors only; drop the third directional (bounce) — one fewer light in every shader, no new shadow casters |
| Contact shadows | Live every frame on desktop, baked on phone | n/a | Keep; noted as candidate if perf regresses |
| Dust motes | 170 additive points, drift under `animateDust` | n/a (subtle) | Keep; already stops under reduced motion |
| Distant furniture | Neutral bed / shelf / chest boxes on the floor | Not visible in boards | Keep, tint to the cool room |
| Colliders / constants | `DESK_*`, `PLATE_*`, `BOOK_*`, `RAMP_*`, `LAMP_*`, `TOY_PROPS` footprints | — | **Unchanged** (tests, `BrickStudioScene` baseplate thickness comment, ScaledEnvironment 64/96/128 all depend on them) |

Direction I details deliberately **not** built: poster text ("SAME DESK MORE WORLDS"), book-spine words, the potted plant
and globe, the mug lettering. Text on props needs a font in a canvas texture and reads as noise at explore scale; a plain
blue poster rectangle stands in for the poster. No post-processing, no extra shadow-casting lights, no new continuous
animation.

## Bounded change list (task 2)

   constant, recolored `TOY_PROPS` (positions / halves / groups / ids unchanged), mug kind gains pencils (visual only).

   `createSkylineTexture`, `createClockFaceTexture`, `createRulerTexture`; warm the wood generator.
3. `ToyRoomWorld.tsx`: blue room + window skyline, butter lamp, brand books with fewer meshes, textured ruler, wall clock,
   pencil mug, recolored dressing, cool/warm light colours, drop the bounce directional. Fog constants recolored.
4. `toyRoom.test.ts`: extend for palette hex validity, clock placement off the desk, mesh-count reductions expressed
   through constants where possible.
5. Perf: before/after with a parameterized copy of `scripts/qa/verify-expanded-performance.mjs` at
   `docs/brand/qa/w7/perf-harness.mjs` (UI_ORIGIN + OUT_DIR env), results in `docs/brand/qa/w7/PERF.md`.

## Media plan (task 3)

Intrinsic sizes (committed early in `public/brand/media/manifest.json` so W2 can lay out placeholders):

- `hero-1600` 1600×960, `hero-1200` 1200×720, `hero-800` 800×480 (5:3) — Toy Room, seed castle, no UI.
- `scene-{toy-room,brick-valley,sky-island,classic}-800` 800×500, `-400` 400×250 (16:10) — same seed build per scene.
- `character-{pip,fern,nova,toy-figure}-400` 400×400 — the real runtime avatar standing on the plate in Toy Room.

Formats: `.webp` (Chrome canvas encoder via Playwright) and `.png`; `.avif` when `avifenc` is on PATH (host tool, no repo
dependency) — otherwise recorded as deferred in the manifest.
