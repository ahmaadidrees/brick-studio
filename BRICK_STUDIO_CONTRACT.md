# Brick Studio vertical-slice contract

## Product promise

A student can open a blank studded baseplate, make a recognizable brick creation in minutes, and then enter the same scene as a third-person character to walk on and around it.

## Included in this slice

- 64 x 64-stud build plate and an original procedural brick library
- Tap/click placement with grid snapping, stacking, overlap feedback, and a placement ghost
- Select, recolor, rotate, move, copy, paste, duplicate, delete, undo, and redo
- Orbit, pan, zoom, reset, and orthographic-style camera shortcuts
- Explore mode with gravity, jumping, third-person camera, and collision against placed bricks
- Responsive desktop, Chromebook, iPad, and phone layouts
- Touch-first placement plus on-screen movement, camera look, and jump controls
- The earlier Rover Island prototype preserved at `/rover`

## Acceptance test

1. Place and stack several different brick shapes without instructions.
2. Recolor, rotate, duplicate, and undo a selected brick.
3. Navigate the build plate comfortably with a mouse and with touch.
4. Enter Explore and walk, jump, collide with, and stand on the creation.
5. Complete the same loop at desktop, tablet, and phone viewport sizes.
6. Keep editing responsive with 200 simple bricks on a representative desktop; apply a lower practical build budget on phones until real-device profiling is complete.

## Deferred

Robotics, Blockly, sensors, motors, classrooms, accounts, multiplayer, imported LEGO geometry, thousands of parts, and persistence are intentionally outside this slice.
