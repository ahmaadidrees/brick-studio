### Decisions for the user / lead

- Display names: board 09 labels the customizable figure "Blocky Builder"; the product ships "Toy Figure" and "Classic
  Builder". Renaming is a one-line descriptor change but breaks the W8 harness locators (`/^Toy Figure/`) and
  the QA doc; not changed without a decision.
- Robot Hero is third-party CC0 art (Quaternius RobotExpressive). It stays in the product (no removals), but marketing
  media per CONTRACTS lists only pip/fern/nova/toy-figure — confirm that W7 should keep it out of marketing.
- Toy Figure default skin tone lives in brick-core `DEFAULT_CHARACTER_APPEARANCE` (frozen, `#f0bd86`); it is a peach
  tone, not toy yellow, so no change is requested.
