# Touch lane: Build stage pinch, touch rules, Fit, bounds (2026-10-31)

Branch `claude/hackable-ux-touch`. Files: `studio/Stage.tsx`, `studio/stage/camera.ts`, new `studio/builder/placement.ts`
(+ tests in `stage/camera.test.ts`, `builder/placement.test.ts`). Verified in headless Chrome on Vite :5191 with real
two-finger CDP touch input (`Input.dispatchTouchEvent`) at Duo single 540×720, iPad landscape 1024×768, iPad portrait
820×1180, and with the mouse at 1400×900. Scripts: scratchpad `touchlane/*.mjs`.

| Item | Status | What changed | How verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| [1·1] Pinch in Build | fixed | The Stage tracks every pointer by `pointerId`. When a second finger lands in Build it undoes whatever the first finger started (paint stroke reverted cell by cell, a brick move put back, the selection restored; placing and erasing a brick wait for the lift on touch, so there's nothing to undo), then zooms with `pinchCamera` (`zoomCameraAt` around the start midpoint plus a 1:1 pan of the midpoint). Fingers still down after a pinch do nothing until all lift. `pointercancel` is handled. Play only lets the finger that started a scroll keep scrolling (Play camera otherwise untouched). | Select, Coin, Ground, Erase at all three touch sizes: pinch 80→240 px = zoom ×3.00 with the world point under the fingers unchanged; two-finger pan of 100 px moves the level exactly 100 px; copies and tiles unchanged, selection unchanged. A pinch whose first finger was dragging a brick leaves the brick where it was. The explorer's `pinch.mjs` (1114×720): no fling, no extra coins or tiles. |
| [1·3] One finger can't scroll with a brick armed | fixed | Two-finger pan works in every tool. Single-finger rule for touch: **a normal brick is placed on a tap (when the finger lifts, moving under 10 px); a drag scrolls the level.** Painting tools keep painting on a drag (Ground paints, Erase erases), because that's what they're for and a kid has two fingers to scroll. Erase removes a placed brick on a tap; a drag that starts on one becomes an erasing stroke. Select: drag a brick to move it, drag empty space to scroll (unchanged). The mouse is unchanged: press places/erases straight away. | Duo: Coin tap +1 copy; Coin one-finger drag 200 px scrolls 200 px, no copy; Ground drag paints 8 cells; Erase drag removes them; Erase tap removes a coin. A cancelled touch with Coin armed places nothing. |
| [1·9] Double-tap jumps to Code | fixed | `onDoubleClick` ignores a double tap from a finger (`pointerType === 'touch'`). Mouse double-click still opens Code. | Duo: double-tap on a coin stays in Build (it's selected). Mouse double-click on a Walker → Code. |
| [1·8] Fit makes bricks 6–9 px | fixed | `buildFitCamera`: Fit shows the whole level when that's at least 0.75 px/step, otherwise zooms to 1 px/step (never past the level's full height), keeping the current spot centred and the view inside the level edges. Play's "Whole level" is unchanged (Play camera is the layout lane's). | Duo single Fit: scale 0.56 → 1.0. 1114×720 and desktop still fit the whole level. |
| [1·7] stage part: selection hidden by the inspector | fixed | After a selection on a small screen, if the brick card (`.cl-slot-inspector`, read at its resting place, ignoring its slide-in transform) covers the bottom of the level, the level scrolls so the selection sits in the middle of the part still showing. When a finger is down it waits until the finger lifts, so it never moves the level mid-drag. Wide screens (card beside the level) never scroll. Bricks button not touched. | Explorer's `insp.mjs` on Duo: Walker at y 670 (card top 364) now scrolls to y 206. |
| [4·5] Ctrl+wheel zooms the page | fixed | The wheel listener is native and `{ passive: false }`, so `preventDefault` works; Safari's trackpad pinch (`gesturestart/change/end`) is cancelled too and zooms the level. Zoom/pan amounts unchanged. | Synthetic Ctrl+wheel on the canvas: `defaultPrevented = true`, level zooms. Plain wheel still pans. (Headless Chrome can't show the page zoom itself.) |
| [4·7] Drags drop at the canvas edge | fixed | `setPointerCapture` on press; pointer leave only ends a drag if capture failed. | Mouse: Hero dragged 80 px past the left edge then back +100 px follows the pointer and lands there. |
| [4·8], [1·18] Bricks/Hero outside the level; Hero stuck in the ground | fixed | `placement.ts`: placing, dragging and arrow-nudging keep the brick's art inside the level (whole grid steps). When the Hero is placed or dropped overlapping painted cells it's lifted to stand on top of them (if there's no clear spot above, it stays). One undo step as before. | Coin clicked in the void above the level lands at its top edge. Hero dragged below the level (mouse and finger) lands standing on the ground (y 24 on one ground row); Ctrl+Z puts it back. Unit tests cover edges, size, walls and a full column. |

Not done / notes:
- Painting ground over a Hero that's already placed can still bury it; only placing and dropping the Hero lifts it. The
  pre-Play check (`playRules.ts`) isn't extended.
- On desktop, selecting a brick still shrinks the stage (the card opens beside it) and re-fits an untouched view, which
  can shift the level under the pointer mid-drag. This was already the case and is the layout lane's.
- Not tried on real devices (Surface Duo, iPad). Pinch was only tested with CDP touch events in headless Chrome. Safari
  gesture events weren't run.
- Tests: `npx tsc -b` clean; `vitest run src/platformer/lab`: everything passes (four files that timed out under load
  pass when run alone).
